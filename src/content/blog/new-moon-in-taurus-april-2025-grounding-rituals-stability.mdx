On April 27, 2025, at approximately 3:31 PM EDT, the Moon slides into perfect alignment with the Sun at 7 degrees Taurus. If you've been feeling scattered, overextended, or vaguely broke — this is your reset. The New Moon in Taurus is the year's most grounding lunation, and it's asking you a single, unfussy question: what do you actually need to feel safe in your own life?

Taurus doesn't rush. Taurus doesn't spiritual-bypass. Taurus wants you to feel the weight of a ceramic mug in your palm, count what's actually in your checking account, and decide — slowly — what deserves to grow over the next six months. This new moon is a planting moon in the most literal, agricultural sense. What you seed on April 27 is what you'll harvest at the Taurus full moon in November.

## What's Actually Happening in the Sky

A new moon happens when the Sun and Moon meet at the same zodiac degree — astronomers call this a syzygy — and for roughly one to three days, the Moon becomes invisible from Earth. It's the darkest point of the 29.5-day lunar cycle, which is exactly why it's traditionally used for setting intentions. You're planting in dark soil.

This particular new moon lands at 7 degrees Taurus, which is about 23% of the way through the sign's 30-degree span — roughly a week into Taurus season, which runs from April 20 to May 20. Taurus is a fixed earth sign ruled by Venus, which means we're working with themes of material security, sensory pleasure, self-worth, and slow, deliberate growth. In traditional astrology, Taurus governs the second house: your money, your possessions, your body, and the quieter question underneath all of them — what you believe you're worth.

The Sabian Symbol for 7 degrees Taurus is "The woman of Samaria at the ancestral well." That image comes from a 1925 collaboration between the American astrologer Marc Edmund Jones and the clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler, who channeled 360 symbols — one for each degree of the zodiac — in a single afternoon in San Diego. Jones interpreted this particular symbol as spiritual nourishment drawn from traditional, inherited sources. Translation: the well is already dug. You don't have to invent a new life. You have to return to what already sustains you.

The last time we had a New Moon in Taurus at a similar degree was April 30, 2022. Cast your mind back. What were you building then? What did you promise yourself you'd take seriously — your body, your savings, a relationship, a creative project — and where did that promise land? This is a three-year echo. The astrologer Dane Rudhyar wrote extensively about Taurus as the archetype of substance-made-visible, the moment when spirit condenses into something you can hold. That's the assignment.

There's older resonance here too. The Roman festival of Floralia ran from April 27 through May 3, honoring Flora, goddess of flowering plants and fertility. The festival opened on the exact date of this year's new moon. Ancient farmers used late April to plant root vegetables and establish garden beds — the unglamorous, foundational work that determines whether you eat in October.

## What This Means for Your Actual Life

**Money and self-worth.** Taurus rules the second house, so financial themes will be loud. This isn't the moon for a get-rich-quick scheme or a wild investment. This is the moon for opening the spreadsheet you've been avoiding, canceling the three subscriptions you forgot about, and having an honest conversation with yourself about what you charge for your work. If you've been undercharging — and you probably have — the two weeks after April 27 are the window to raise your rates.

**Body and pleasure.** Taurus is the most embodied sign of the zodiac. Under this moon, your nervous system wants slow food, warm baths, textured fabrics, and sleep that goes past 7 AM. If you've been running on cold brew and adrenaline since January, this is the correction. Book the massage. Cook something that takes three hours. Put your bare feet on actual grass.

**Relationships.** Venus rules Taurus, so partnership themes are lit up — but in a specifically material way. This is less "define the relationship" and more "who's paying for what, and does it feel fair?" If you live with someone, the new moon is a good week to talk about the domestic labor split without turning it into a fight. Taurus energy makes hard conversations more possible because it moves slowly and stays calm.

**Home and possessions.** Look around your space. What are you keeping out of obligation rather than love? Taurus doesn't want minimalism for its own sake — Taurus wants a home full of things you actually enjoy touching. If your couch makes you sad, this new moon is asking why you're still sitting on it.

**Career.** Postpone signing major contracts until after May 7, when Mercury has fully cleared its post-retrograde shadow. If you're negotiating salary, gather your data first — comparable roles, market rates, your specific wins from the last twelve months. Taurus rewards preparation over improvisation.

## 6 Grounding Rituals for the Taurus New Moon

**1. Plant something on April 27.** Literally. Buy a packet of basil seeds, a small pot of soil, or a rosemary start from the farmers' market. Taurus season historically marks the planting of root vegetables in agricultural calendars, and the physical act of pressing a seed into dirt with your thumb activates the second-house themes of tangible growth. Water it on the same day every week and watch what happens by the June solstice.

**2. Do a "worth" audit before May 4.** Sit down with a pen — not a phone, a pen — and write down every source of income you have, every recurring expense, and every service or skill you provide for free that other people charge for. You're not trying to become ruthless. You're trying to see clearly. Taurus rules the second house, and the second house rewards honesty about numbers.

**3. Build an altar with earth, rose, and vanilla.** Traditional Taurus correspondences include rose, vanilla, and patchouli scents, plus the colors green and pink. On the evening of April 27, gather a small dish of soil, a pink or green candle, a fresh rose or lilac stem, and something that represents an intention — a coin for money, a key for home, a photograph for love. Let it sit for the three days the Moon is dark.

**4. Take a two-hour sensory walk.** Leave your phone at home. Walk somewhere with trees, water, or open sky, and notice five things you can touch, four things you can smell, three sounds, two tastes if you brought a snack, and one thing that moves you. This isn't productive. It's not supposed to be. Dane Rudhyar described Taurus as the sign that teaches presence through the senses — this is the practical version.

**5. Cook one slow, foundational meal between April 27 and May 3.** Bread. A pot of beans from dried. A braise that takes four hours. Something where you can smell it changing across the day. This overlaps with the Roman Floralia festival window and honors the older, agricultural rhythm underneath all the astrological theory. Taurus wants to be fed, not fueled.

**6. Write a letter to yourself from three years ago.** The last New Moon in Taurus at this degree was April 30, 2022. What did that version of you want? What promises did they make? Write them a short letter — a page, no more — telling them what you kept, what you dropped, and what you're planting now. If you want a bonus prompt, [break a fortune cookie](/) after you finish and let whatever appears be the closing line.

**7. Rest without guilt for one full afternoon.** Not "rest while answering emails." Not "rest while listening to a productivity podcast." Actual rest. Taurus is a fixed sign, which means it doesn't like being rushed, and your body will tell you exactly what it needs if you stop drowning it out. If you want a small anchor, check [your horoscope](/horoscope) for the day and let it be the only external input you take in.

**8. Name one thing you'll no longer give away for free.** This could be your time, your emotional labor, your professional advice, or your weekends. Say it out loud on the night of April 27. Write it on a small piece of paper and tuck it into your wallet. Taurus is the sign of value, and value only exists when there's a clear line between yes and no.

## A Grounding Close

Here's the honest part. New moons are real in the sense that lunar cycles have shaped human behavior for as long as we've been keeping records, and they're real in the sense that intention-setting works whether or not you believe the sky is watching. Maybe the cosmos is participating. Maybe you're just finally giving yourself permission to slow down. Both can be true.

The New Moon in Taurus isn't asking you to overhaul your life. It's asking you to notice the well that's already there, drink from it, and plant something small enough to actually tend. Go to bed early on April 27. Wake up on April 28 and do one gentle thing. That's the whole ritual.
