On March 29, 2025, at 6:58 AM EDT, the Moon slips into perfect alignment with the Sun at 8 degrees Aries — the first new moon of the astrological new year, and arguably the most electric lunar reset you'll get all cycle. If you've been waiting for a signal to start the thing, send the message, quit the job, or finally book the flight, this is it. The takeaway in one line: pick the boldest version of what you want and take one visible step toward it before March 31.

This isn't a soft, contemplative new moon. Aries doesn't do contemplation. It does spark, ignition, the sharp crisp sensation of breaking through resistance — the astrological equivalent of biting into something spicy and feeling your whole face wake up. What follows is what's actually happening in the sky, why it matters for your regular Tuesday-morning life, and eight specific moves to make between now and early April.

## What's Actually Happening on March 29

A new moon happens when the Sun and Moon occupy the exact same degree of the zodiac — in this case, 8 degrees of Aries. For roughly 3.5 days around that conjunction, the Moon is invisible to the naked eye. If you step outside on the night of March 28 or 29 in a place with low light pollution, the sky goes fully, dramatically black. No silver crescent, no gibbous glow. Just stars. Ancient Babylonian astronomers were tracking exactly this phase as early as 1800 BCE, marking new moons on clay tablets as moments of civic and agricultural renewal — a fresh page in the ledger of the year.

Aries is ruled by Mars, named for the Roman god of war, and Mars is the planet of action, courage, and initiation. So when the lunar reset lands in Aries, you get compounded starter energy: the Moon's monthly restart button pressed inside the sign that already governs beginnings. The astrological new year technically began at the spring equinox on March 20, when the Sun first entered Aries, but this new moon is the first lunar reset of the fresh zodiacal cycle. Think of the equinox as the sunrise and this new moon as your first real cup of coffee.

There's one wrinkle worth naming. Mars — the ruler of this whole show — is currently traveling through Cancer, a sign that cares deeply about home, family, and emotional safety. That creates an interesting tension: your instinct to charge ahead will be shadowed by a quieter voice asking whether it's safe, whether the people you love will still be there, whether you can protect what you already have. Don't dismiss that voice. It's not fear, exactly. It's the strategist making sure your bold move doesn't burn down the house.

Humanistic astrologer Dane Rudhyar, writing in the 1930s and 40s, framed the lunation cycle as a spiritual process — not a monthly ritual to perform, but a living arc of intention and release across 29.5 days. Under Rudhyar's lens, the new moon isn't when you write your wish list. It's when you plant the seed of who you're becoming across the next month. The Aries version of that seed is almost always: who are you when you stop asking permission?

The last new moon at similar degrees of Aries occurred in early April 2024. Look back at that spring. A lot of people I know pivoted careers, launched things, ended relationships that had been quietly finished for months. The pattern was rarely "made a plan and executed" — it was more often "woke up one Tuesday and knew."

## What This Means for Your Actual Life

In the natural zodiac wheel, Aries rules the first house — the house of self, body, identity, and how you show up in a room. So this new moon puts a spotlight on you specifically: your face, your posture, your name on the door.

**Career and money.** If you've been drafting a resignation email in your head for six months, this is the week the draft becomes the send. Aries new moons favor the person who acts before they feel ready. That doesn't mean quit without a plan — the Mars-in-Cancer influence wants you to have a soft landing — but it does mean stop waiting for a perfect moment that isn't coming.

**Relationships.** Because Aries is the "I" sign, this transit tends to surface the question of whether you've been shrinking to fit. If you've been avoiding a conversation with a partner, roommate, or parent about what you actually need, the 48-hour window after 6:58 AM EDT on March 29 is when the words will come out most cleanly. Expect them to feel a little too direct. That's the point.

**Body and health.** Aries governs the head, brain, and face in medical astrology. When this energy gets blocked — when you swallow the anger, delay the decision, sit on the impulse — it tends to show up as tension headaches, jaw clenching, or breakouts along the hairline and forehead. If you notice any of these in the last days of March, the fix is usually motion, not medication: a walk, a hard workout, a conversation you've been postponing.

**Creativity.** New starts under Aries respond to physicality. Painting, cooking, dancing, building something with your hands — anything that involves your body making a mark. This is not the moon for staring at a blank Google Doc. Get up.

**Home.** The Mars-in-Cancer piece means your home base matters more than usual. Before you launch the bold thing, make sure your kitchen is stocked, your bed is made, your closest people know what you're up to. The launchpad has to be solid.

## 8 Bold Moves to Make Before April 5

**1. Write one sentence on the morning of March 29 that begins "I am becoming."** Do this before 3:00 PM EDT to catch the peak 8-hour manifestation window. Keep it present-tense and specific — "I am becoming someone who leaves at 5" beats "I want work-life balance." Fold the paper and put it somewhere you'll see it daily for the next 29.5 days.

**2. Send the message you've been drafting.** The one to the ex, the old boss, the friend you ghosted, the collaborator you admire. Aries rewards the person who hits send. If it helps, [break a fortune cookie](/) first and let the slip decide your opening line.

**3. Book one physical thing for the first week of April.** A boxing class, a hike, a haircut that changes how your face looks in photos. Aries lives in the body, especially the head and face. Change something visible.

**4. Make a list of three things you've been asking permission for — and stop.** Not confrontationally. Just quietly. Notice how often you check in with someone about a decision that is entirely yours. Between March 29 and April 5, make three small unilateral choices without running them by anyone.

**5. Eat something spicy on the night of March 29.** This sounds like a joke and isn't. Aries corresponds to the taste of heat — chili, ginger, black pepper, mustard. Anchoring a lunar intention to a physical sensation makes it stick. You'll remember the meal.

**6. Check your [lucky numbers](/lucky-numbers) and use them.** Aries new moons favor small, low-stakes risks: a lottery ticket, a raffle, a coin flip on which restaurant. Not because the universe owes you a jackpot, but because practicing the muscle of "I'll take the chance" is exactly what this transit is training.

**7. Have the money conversation.** Ask for the raise, name the rate, tell your partner what the actual credit card balance is. Aries hates hedging, and this new moon will make hedging feel physically uncomfortable. Use that. The window between March 30 and April 2 is particularly clean for financial directness.

**8. Read your [Aries daily horoscope](/horoscope/daily/aries) — even if you're not an Aries.** Every chart has Aries somewhere. Whichever house it falls in for you is the area of life getting the wake-up call this week. If you don't know your houses, just read the Aries forecast and translate: wherever it says "you," ask where in your life you've been playing small.

## One Last Thing

Cosmic events are real. So is the fact that you're a person who needs to sleep, drink water, and not overhaul your entire life in a single weekend because Mercury did a thing. The Aries new moon isn't asking you to become someone new by April. It's asking you to take one honest step in the direction of who you already suspect you are.

If that step is small — a text, a walk, a sentence written on a folded piece of paper — it still counts. The spark doesn't have to be a bonfire. It just has to catch.
