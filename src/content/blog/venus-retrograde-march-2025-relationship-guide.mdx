Venus stations retrograde on March 1, 2025, at 10° Aries, then slips back into Pisces on March 27 before stationing direct on April 12. If you've been circling the same question about a relationship — stay, go, speak up, pull back — the next six weeks are built for exactly that reckoning. One-line takeaway: don't start anything new with someone whose last name you just learned, and don't end anything old without sleeping on it three times first.

This is the first Venus retrograde since July 22 to September 3, 2023, when Venus walked backward through Leo and a lot of us ended up texting people we swore we'd blocked. The 2025 cycle moves through fire and water — Aries first (the part of you that wants, directly, now), then Pisces (the part of you that still dreams about someone from 2017). That mix is why this one feels less like a tidy breakup season and more like an emotional archaeology dig.

## What's actually happening up there

Venus doesn't literally reverse course. It's an optical trick, the same one that makes a slower car on the highway look like it's drifting backward when you overtake it. Venus completes its orbit around the Sun every 225 Earth days, moving faster than we do. When it laps Earth on the inside track, our perspective makes it appear to loop backward through the zodiac for roughly 40 to 43 days before resuming forward motion. This happens about every 18 months, which makes Venus retrograde one of the rarer retrogrades — Mercury, by comparison, does this three or four times a year without anyone noticing until their flight gets delayed.

The Babylonians were tracking this cycle before most of Europe had a written alphabet. Their astronomers logged Venus's appearances as both the Morning Star and the Evening Star on clay tablets, and they could predict the retrograde periods centuries ahead. They treated these moments as omens — not casual ones, either. The Maya built entire sections of the Dresden Codex around Venus's synodic cycle of 584 days, timing military decisions and royal ceremonies to its stations.

Then the Hellenistic astrologers inherited the planet, renamed its energy after Aphrodite, and tied Venus to love, beauty, art, money, and the aesthetic sense that makes you rearrange your living room at 11 p.m. Venus still rules two signs today: Taurus, which governs the body and material pleasure, and Libra, which governs partnership and fairness. When Venus goes retrograde, both of those domains get a review period.

Here's the pattern worth knowing: Venus retrogrades trace a near-perfect five-pointed star across the zodiac over an 8-year cycle. The retrograde you're living through in March 2025 is a near-echo of the one from March and April 2017, which also began in Aries. Think back. What were you negotiating in your love life or your bank account around spring 2017? The themes rhyme, even if the characters have changed. Ancient Greek culture considered this 8-year rhythm sacred enough to anchor the Olympic Games cycle to it — a reminder that cosmic timing was once as ordinary as a calendar on the fridge.

Venus is also the brightest object in the night sky after the Moon right now, and during retrograde its apparent brightness actually increases as it swings closer to Earth. Step outside after sunset in early March and you'll see it blazing in the west. The sky is showing you the transit.

## What this means for your actual life

**Relationships you're already in.** This isn't a destruction cycle. It's a renovation cycle. Existing partnerships that can handle honest reassessment tend to come out stronger. The conversations that feel scary to start — about money, about sex, about whether you both still want the same shape of life — are exactly the ones this transit supports. You're not being asked to blow anything up. You're being asked to look at it in daylight.

**New romance.** Astrological tradition is pretty consistent on this: relationships that begin during Venus retrograde tend to face unusual turbulence or require significant rework once Venus goes direct. The person who seems perfect on March 10 may feel like a stranger on April 20. This doesn't mean don't date. It means don't make vows, don't move in, don't get the matching tattoo until mid-May at the earliest.

**Exes.** They will text. You will think about texting them. Someone from an unresolved chapter will cross your mind at the grocery store and you'll suddenly remember the smell of their jacket. This is the Pisces portion of the retrograde doing its work — Venus moves back into Pisces on March 27, and the water sign dissolves the emotional borders you'd carefully drawn. Reconnecting isn't automatically a mistake. Reconnecting without asking why is.

**Money and beauty.** Venus rules both. Expect to question a subscription, reconsider a style you've been performing for years, maybe fall out of love with a color scheme in your apartment. Big financial commitments made during these six weeks often get renegotiated later. Hold off on the dramatic haircut until April 12 — or at least don't do it the week Venus stations.

**Self-worth.** The deepest layer. Venus retrograde brings up the old question of what you believe you deserve. Pay attention to the moments you flinch when someone compliments you, or the moments you accept treatment you'd never recommend to a friend. Those flinches are the material.

## 6 things to actually do between March 1 and April 12

1. **Make a list on March 2 of the five relationships that matter most to you right now.** Include friendships and family, not just romance. Next to each name, write one sentence about what's working and one about what isn't. Revisit the list on April 13, the day after Venus stations direct, and see what's shifted. This gives the transit something concrete to work on instead of letting it fog around in your chest.

2. **Postpone major cosmetic changes until after April 12.** Hair color, tattoos, Botox, dramatic wardrobe overhauls. Venus rules aesthetics, and retrograde-era choices have a high regret rate. If you absolutely must do something, pick the reversible version — a wig before the chop, a henna before the ink.

3. **Have the money conversation you've been dodging in the first two weeks of March.** Whether it's with a partner about shared expenses, with a parent about an old loan, or with yourself about what you're actually spending on delivery apps. Venus rules finances as much as it rules love, and the early retrograde window is where honesty lands cleanest.

4. **If an ex contacts you between March 27 and April 12, wait 48 hours before responding.** This is the Pisces portion, when boundaries get watery and nostalgia feels like truth. Forty-eight hours is enough time for the fog to lift. If you still want to reply after two days, reply. If the urge has passed, you've learned something.

5. **Buy nothing over $300 impulsively.** Venus retrograde is famous for buyer's remorse. Add a 72-hour cooling-off rule to any purchase above that threshold through April 12. The couch, the jacket, the course, the plane ticket to visit someone you're ambivalent about — all of it benefits from three days of silence before you commit.

6. **Spend one evening in early April revisiting a creative project you abandoned.** Venus governs art and craft, and the retrograde is uniquely good for returning to the unfinished. The novel in the drawer, the painting you started during lockdown, the song with no second verse. Don't force completion. Just open the file and sit with it for an hour. Something will tell you whether it still wants to be made.

7. **Pull a [daily fortune](/daily) or [break a fortune cookie](/) on the two station days — March 1 and April 12.** These are the hinge moments of the transit, when Venus pauses before changing direction. Whatever message surfaces on those days tends to carry unusual weight. Write it down. Check it against your life six weeks later.

## A grounding note before you go

Venus retrograde is real in the sense that the sky is doing what the sky is doing, and humans have been noticing for at least four thousand years. It's also just six weeks. You will not be transformed into someone unrecognizable. You will probably have one hard conversation, reconsider one purchase, dream about one person you hadn't thought about in a while, and emerge in mid-April with slightly clearer edges around what you actually want. Drink water. Sleep more than you think you need. The planets will keep moving whether or not you optimize for them, and the best thing you can bring to any transit is a rested nervous system and a willingness to tell yourself the truth.
